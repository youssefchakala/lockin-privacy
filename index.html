LockIn Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

LockIn is a focus app for Android. It blocks short-video feeds (Instagram and Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts, the TikTok feed and, if you choose, all of YouTube) while a focus session you start or schedule is running. This policy explains what data LockIn uses and why.

Accessibility service

LockIn uses Android's Accessibility service, only after you switch it on, to see which screen is open in Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, TikTok Studio and your web browser. It uses this to:

  • recognise a Reels, Shorts, feed video, TikTok feed or YouTube screen (or the matching website) and close or cover it during a focus session;
  • open TikTok's inbox instead of its video feed;
  • cover videos in the Instagram home feed, if you turn that option on.

Screen content is checked on your phone, at the moment it is shown, and is then discarded. LockIn does not read or store your messages, does not record your screen, does not keep a history of what you viewed and does not send any of it anywhere.

Data stored on your phone

LockIn stores your settings, your scheduled sessions, the current session's timer, your focus stats (streak, total focus time, number of sessions), your garden (coins, plants and the items you unlocked) and the name you enter (optional) in its own private storage on your phone. Apart from the account and friends features and the usage statistics below, none of this leaves your phone. Streak reminders and the home-screen widget are made on the phone from this data; nothing is sent to show them.

If Android backup is switched on for your Google account, Android includes your focus stats, garden and name in your phone's own encrypted backup, so they come back when you reinstall LockIn or move to a new phone. This backup is handled by Google under your account, and LockIn cannot read it. Settings, schedules and the session timer are never backed up. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Account (optional)

You can sign in with your Google account in Settings, so your progress comes back if you reinstall LockIn or change phones. LockIn then receives your Google account's email address and an account ID from Google (through Firebase Authentication), and stores a copy of your progress under that ID: your coins, garden, plants, items, streak and focus totals, your name and your friend code. Only you, signed in with that Google account, can read it. It is used for nothing else, never sold and never used for ads.

You can sign out at any time (your progress stays on the phone), or delete your account with "Delete my account" in Settings. That deletes the account, the saved progress and your friends data from the server. You can also ask for deletion at the contact address below.

Friends

LockIn lets you add friends with a friend code and see each other's progress. Together with the optional account above, the usage statistics and the ads below, it is the only part of LockIn that sends data off your phone. It uses Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), with the data stored in Google's European data centres. To keep fake copies of the app from abusing this server, the Google Play version of LockIn also uses Firebase App Check: Google Play checks that requests come from the real LockIn installed from Google Play on a genuine device, and Firebase receives only the yes-or-no result, not your identity. The Google Play version also reads one public setting from this server, the oldest LockIn version still supported, so it can ask you to update through Google Play; this read sends nothing about you, and it is skipped after "Delete my friends data".

The first time LockIn opens with the friends feature, your phone signs in anonymously: LockIn creates a random account ID, without asking for your email, phone number or any login. LockIn then stores, under that ID: the name you entered in LockIn (or "LockIn friend" if you didn't enter one), your friend code, your current and best streak, today's and your total focus time, your number of sessions and coins, how many plants, pots, animals and items you have, the plant you're growing, a picture-free description of your garden (which plants, pots, decorations and animals stand where) so friends can see it in 3D, your focus time this week, and the time your current focus session ends, if one is running. It also stores which friend codes you've added.

Only people who have your friend code, or whose code you added, can see this information. It can't be searched or listed. If you report a friend's name, LockIn stores the report (your account ID, theirs, and the reported name) so it can be reviewed. Removing a friend also blocks them: they don't show up in your list again, even if they add your code, unless you add theirs.

Groups. You can create a group or join one with its code. LockIn then stores the group's name, its code, who made it and the account IDs of its members (at most 20). Only the group's members can see it; they see each other's name, streak, focus time this week and whether they're focusing now. You can leave a group at any time; when the person who made it deletes it, or the last member leaves, it is removed from the server.

Focusing together. When you lock in with friends or a group, LockIn stores the session: your account ID, the account IDs of the people you invited, its length, who joined the waiting room, who is ready, who said no or left, and when it ends once it starts. The people invited see it and can join. LockIn deletes it from the server when the session ends or the waiting room is cancelled or closes (after 15 minutes). To show invites, LockIn listens for them while it runs and can show a notification (only if you allow notifications).

Invite links. The message you share can include a link to a page on this website with your friend code in it. Opening it opens LockIn (or Google Play) with that code filled in; nothing is added until the person taps Add. When LockIn is installed from such a link, Google Play passes the code to LockIn once (its "install referrer"); LockIn reads only the friend code from it and keeps it on the phone until it is used.

Bringing a friend. If you are new to LockIn and add a friend's code, LockIn remembers on your phone whose code it was. When you finish your 3rd focus session, it stores on the server your account ID, your friend's account ID and whether your friend has collected their bonus, so each of you gets the coins once. Only the two of you can see it, and either of you can delete it.

You can delete all of this at any time with "Delete my friends data" in LockIn's Settings. That removes your profile, your friend code, your friendships, the records of friends you brought or who brought you, and the anonymous account from the server; nothing is sent again unless you share your code or add a friend later. Uninstalling LockIn stops updates, but leaves your last profile on the server until you ask for it to be deleted at the contact address below. Firebase's handling of this data is covered by the Firebase privacy information.

Usage statistics

To learn which parts of LockIn help people and whether they keep using it, LockIn uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It sends a few events about how the app is used: a focus session started (its length, and whether it was started by hand, by a schedule or with friends), finished or broken, an objective claimed, a plant that grew a stage, a reward video watched for the daily gift or to double coins, a bonus for bringing a friend, whether Premium is on, the first-time setup steps reached (welcome shown, tour started, blocker explained, blocker on, first session started and finished, notifications allowed or not), a streak freeze bought, a streak goal set or reached, a streak earned back, and a friend's invite link used. Because LockIn's ads come from Google AdMob, Google Analytics also receives, for each ad shown, its format and the revenue it earned, so we can see what the free version earns. Google Analytics also records its own basic information: that the app was opened or installed, a random app-instance ID, your phone model, Android version, app version, language and approximate country (from the IP address), and purchases made through Google Play.

LockIn never sends your name, friend code, messages, the apps or videos you open, or anything you type to Google Analytics. Analytics does not collect your advertising ID, and this data is not used for ads or ad personalisation. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Analytics only stores its app-instance ID on your phone if you agreed to storage on your device in LockIn's consent form ("Privacy and ad settings"); otherwise it only receives anonymous, cookie-less signals. Google keeps this data for 2 months, then deletes it. It is covered by the Firebase privacy information.

Ads

The free version of LockIn shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), app interactions with ads, and diagnostic information. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, LockIn asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from "Privacy and ad settings" in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings (Google → Ads).

Purchases

The optional "LockIn Premium" removes ads (as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time purchase), and coin packs add coins to your garden. Payments are processed by Google Play. LockIn never sees your payment details; it only receives confirmation from Google Play that the subscription is active or that a purchase was made.

Refunds

You can cancel a subscription at any time in Google Play. Cancelling stops the next payment, and Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for. If your subscription started with a free trial, cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged; otherwise the first payment is taken when the trial ends.

Premium monthly: you can ask for a refund within 7 days of the payment, and each renewal is its own payment. After 7 days it is not refundable. A refunded Premium is switched off.

Premium yearly or forever: you can ask for a refund within 14 days of the payment, and each yearly renewal is its own payment. After 14 days it is not refundable. A refunded Premium is switched off.

Coin packs: not refundable once the coins are added, unless they were not delivered or don't work.

How to ask: in the first 48 hours, ask Google Play. After that, email tekbound.tech@gmail.com with your Google Play order number (GPA...).

These limits don't apply where the law gives you more rights (for example for a faulty product or a payment you didn't authorise), or where Google Play grants a refund under its own rules.

Deleting your account and data

To delete your LockIn account and everything stored for it online:

  1. In LockIn, open the Settings tab (the gear in the bottom bar) and tap "Delete my account". Confirm with your Google account. This deletes the account, your saved progress, your friend profile and code, your friendships and your friend bonus records.
  2. If you only use friends without an account, tap "Delete my friends data" in Settings instead.
  3. If you no longer have the app, email tekbound.tech@gmail.com from the address of your Google account (or with your friend code) and ask for deletion. It is done within 30 days.

Deleted data is removed from the server straight away and is not kept. Reports you made about other people's names are kept until they are reviewed. The data on your phone stays until you uninstall LockIn.
